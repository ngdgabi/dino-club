# Dino Club: Stone Age Feast

**Native 120Hz Metal Slapstick Caveman Platformer & Giant Dino Feast Adventure**  
*Universal Purchase for macOS 14+, iOS 16+, and iPadOS 16+ (`com.ngd.dinoclub`)*

Inspired by the hilarious 1991/1993 classic prehistoric platformers — built from scratch in Swift & SpriteKit with **100% original IP, procedural cartoon artwork, and dual-chip real-time audio synthesis**.

---

## Five Prehistoric Worlds, Sky Glider Bonus & Live 1991 Time-Machine

1. **Boga the Hungry Caveman & Club Upgrades**: Swing your knobby **Oak Club**, upgrade to the **Granite Stone Mallet** & **Mega Bone Hammer**, and unleash **Jump + Down + Club Ground-Pounds** to shake secret **Mega-Croissants** from tree canopies and smash cracked stone caches!
2. **The Iconic Feast Meter (`0% -> 100% FULL BELLY!`)**: Gobble oversized **Mammoth Drumsticks, Stone-Grilled Burgers, Giant Pineapples, Honeycombs, Golden Bananas, Glacier Sundaes & Mega-Croissants** plus **Stone Wheels & Amber Gems** to unlock each stage's Exit Cave!
3. **5 Distinct Worlds + King Chomper T-Rex Boss**: Traverse *Palm Jungle Canyon*, *Amber Cavern Grotto*, *Magma Geyser Ridge*, *Glacier Mammoth Peak*, and the *Volcano Rex Arena* showdown!
4. **Bamboo Hang-Glider Sky Feast Bonus**: Swoop over smoking volcanoes through floating fruit rings and amber gems!
5. **Instant 1991 ↔ 2026 Time-Machine**: Press **`V`** & **`B`** (or tap **`ERA`** & **`CHIP`**) anytime to morph live between **120Hz HD Cartoon Art + Tropical Marimba Synth**, **CRT Arcade Cabinet**, and **16-Color 1991 VGA Pixel Art + AdLib YM3812 FM Chiptunes**!

## Build & Run

```bash
# Build Universal 2 (Apple Silicon arm64 + Intel x86_64) App, DMG & ZIP:
make release

# Launch on macOS:
open "dist/Dino Club.app"

# Verify macOS & iOS/iPadOS Xcode Release targets:
make macos
make ios
```
